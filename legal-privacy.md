## What Hest collects

Hest is non-custodial and does not ask for your name, email address or identity documents to trade.

* **Wallet address** – the public address you connect, and the on-chain activity tied to it on Hest (orders, positions, deposits, vault shares). This is needed to show you your account.
* **Usage events** – which pages and markets you view, orders you place, features you use. Used to run the service and improve it.
* **Technical data** – device type, approximate country derived from your connection, timestamps. Hest stores a salted hash of a visitor identifier, **not your IP address**.
* **Copilot messages** – questions you send to Copilot and the market context attached to them, processed to generate the answer.

## What Hest does not collect

No seed phrases, no private keys, no identity documents, no contact list, no browsing outside hest.si.

## Third parties

* Hyperliquid receives the orders you route to order book markets, signed by your wallet, and holds the related positions and margin under its own terms and privacy policy.
* Wallet connection and optional email/Google login are provided by the wallet infrastructure partner named in the connect dialog, under its own policy.
* Infrastructure (hosting, database, CDN) processes data on Hest's behalf under contract.

Hest does not sell personal data and does not run advertising.

## Retention

Account and trading records are kept while your address has activity on Hest and as required by law. Usage events are aggregated and the raw rows deleted on a rolling basis.

## Your choices

You can disconnect your wallet at any time. Because wallet addresses and on-chain records are public by nature, Hest cannot delete on-chain history, but it can delete the off-chain records tied to your address on request through Discord.
