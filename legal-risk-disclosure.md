# Risk disclosure

Trading perpetual contracts with leverage carries a high level of risk and is not suitable for everyone. Before using Hest, read this page in full.

## Loss of capital

Leverage amplifies both gains and losses. A position can be liquidated by ordinary market movement, and the margin backing it will be lost. Memecoins in particular can lose most of their value in hours. Never trade with funds you cannot afford to lose.

## Market and liquidity risk

Prices of digital assets are volatile. Order book markets can gap; thin markets can have wide spreads and slippage. Hest Pool markets are priced on a time-weighted average and settle against a vault; large or fast moves affect the vault's ability to pay.

## Vault deposits

Depositing into a Hest Pool vault means taking the opposite side of that market's traders. Vault value can fall, including to zero, when traders are profitable. Fees do not guarantee a positive return.

## Technology risk

Hest relies on smart contracts, oracles, Hyperliquid (the third-party venue where order book markets are executed and margined) and network infrastructure. Bugs, outages, oracle failures or network congestion can cause losses, delayed settlement or the inability to open or close positions.

## Super Intelligence

SI Scores, Risk Shield verdicts, Copilot answers and project notes are informational outputs of automated analysis. They can be wrong, late or incomplete. They are not investment advice and do not predict prices.

## Beta

Hest is in Beta. Features, limits, fees and parameters may change, and services may be interrupted, with notice given on the official channels.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for the security of your wallet, for complying with the laws of your jurisdiction, and for every order you sign. Hest cannot reverse transactions.

If you do not understand or do not accept these risks, do not use Hest.
