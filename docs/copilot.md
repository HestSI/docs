# Copilot

Copilot is the part of Super Intelligence you can ask questions. It lives in two places: the **Super Intelligence** page (any market) and under **Risk Shield** on every trade page (this market and the order you are drafting).

## What It Answers

Every answer is built from live numbers for the market you are looking at:

* **Leverage safety**: your liquidation distance against the worst candle of the week, and a safer leverage if yours is too tight.
* **Funding and crowding**: which side is paying funding, and open interest against volume.
* **The worst candle**: the largest 1-hour drop of the past week.
* **Fees** for the market and route.
* **The score**: why the market has its SI Score, its strongest and weakest factor.

## What to Ask

The quick buttons cover the common questions: **What do you think?**, **Is this safe at 10x?**, **Is the long side crowded?**, **Worst candle this week?**, **What are the fees?** On a trade page: **Is this size safe?**, **Why this score?**, **Safer leverage?** You can also type your own question.

## Risk Reads, Not Price Calls

Copilot reads risk. It does not tell you where the price is going, and it does not tell you to buy or sell. If something is not in the data, it says so.

{% hint style="info" %}
Copilot is information, not financial advice. It reads the market; the decision is yours.
{% endhint %}
