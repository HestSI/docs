# Earnings

Your **Earnings** balance holds what Hest owes you on top of your own funds: **profits from Hest Pools** and any **bonuses**. It is shown as Locked Earnings in Portfolio.

## How It Works

1. **Credited.** When a Hest Pools position closes in profit, the profit is credited to Earnings. Your collateral returns to your Trading Wallet at the same time. Bonuses, and a market opener's fee share, are credited the same way.
2. **Locked for 7 days.** Each amount is locked for 7 days from the moment it is credited, with a countdown.
3. **Reviewed.** After the lock, Hest reviews the trades behind it.
4. **Paid.** Hest pays it out and you get the transaction link.

## Why the Lock

Hest Pools markets trade young memecoins against Hest, priced on DEX pools. The 7-day lock and review are the last line of defence against manipulation: profits from a pushed pool, from linked accounts trading against each other or from a broken price can be withheld.

## What It Is Not

* **Not in your Trading Wallet yet.** Your deposits and the collateral of closed positions are in your Trading Wallet and can be withdrawn at any time. Earnings are paid by Hest after the lock and review.
* **Not on Order Book markets.** Profit on Order Book markets is settled by Hyperliquid in your Trading Wallet's Hyperliquid account straight away. Nothing is locked.
