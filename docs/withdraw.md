# Withdraw

Withdrawals go **only to your connected wallet**: the wallet you signed in with. You never type a destination address, so a withdrawal cannot be sent anywhere else. **Hest charges no withdrawal fee.**

## How to Withdraw

1. Click **Withdraw** in Portfolio (or in the wallet menu at the top right).
2. Choose the side: **Hest Pools** or **Order Book**.
3. Enter the amount, or click **Max**.
4. Click **Review Withdrawal**. Hest shows what you withdraw, every fee, what you receive, on which network, and how long it takes.
5. Click **Confirm Withdrawal**. The dialog follows it until it arrives, with a transaction link.

## Hest Pools Side (Robinhood Chain)

* Withdraw **ETH** or **WETH** from your Trading Wallet.
* **Receive On**: Robinhood Chain (to your connected wallet), or **Arbitrum, Ethereum or Base**, bridged by Relay and delivered as ETH. WETH is unwrapped to ETH before it is bridged.
* A small amount of ETH stays in the Trading Wallet for network fees.

## Order Book Side (Hyperliquid)

* USDC from your Hyperliquid account, delivered to your connected wallet **on Arbitrum**.
* Hyperliquid charges **1 USDC** per withdrawal. The minimum is **2 USDC**.
* It takes **about 5 minutes**.

## What You Cannot Withdraw Yet

* Margin that backs open positions or resting orders.
* Your [Earnings](earnings.md) balance while it is locked. Locked profits and bonuses are paid by Hest after their 7-day lock and review.
