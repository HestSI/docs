# Referrals

Every Hest account has a personal referral code and link. Invite people with it and you earn a share of their trading fees, for as long as they trade.

## Your Code and Link

Your referral code is your first username, in lowercase, and your link is `hest.si/r/<code>`. Both are in **Portfolio → Referrals**, next to the number of people who joined with your link and what you have earned so far. Your code stays the same even if you later change your username.

Share the link any way you like. The Referrals card can also draw your personal **referral ticket**, an image with your code on it: copy or download it, or click **Post Your Referral Ticket** to open a ready-made post on X with the ticket on your clipboard.

## What You Earn

* **10% of the Hest Pools trading fees** of everyone who joins through your link. Hest Pools charges 0.30% to open and 0.30% to close a position; every time one of your invitees pays such a fee, 10% of it is credited to you. The share comes out of Hest's part of the fee, so your invitees pay nothing extra.
* Referral earnings are credited in WETH, appear under **Earnings** in Portfolio as "Referral fee share", and follow the same rules as every other earning: **locked for 7 days**, then reviewed by Hest and paid manually, with the transaction link shown when it is paid.
* There is no limit on how many people you can invite or on how much you can earn.

## How Someone Counts as Your Invitee

A new account counts as yours when its **first sign-in** happens after opening your link. The link is remembered in that person's browser, so they can look around first and sign in later. Accounts that already exist cannot be claimed, an account cannot refer itself, and an attribution never changes afterwards.

{% hint style="info" %}
Earnings come from real trading fees, so they vary with how much your invitees actually trade. Nothing here is a guaranteed return.
{% endhint %}
