# Your First Hest Loan

Every account that logs in to hest.si draws a **Hest Loan**: a trading balance in dollars, assigned to the account, ready to use on Hest Pools. It costs nothing and needs no deposit.

Before trading opened, the same loan came with a numbered whitelist ticket. Those accounts keep both the loan and the **Early Supporter** badge, which shows next to the name on the site and in Discord.

## How to Get It

On [hest.si](https://hest.si), click **Log In** and sign in with a wallet or with Google. The loan is drawn and shown to you right there. A few short steps follow:

1. **Follow @HestSI** on X.
2. **Join Our Discord.**
3. **Repost Our Post** on X.
4. **Reply to the Post** on X. Your launch card is in this step: copy or download it first, then send the reply with the card attached.
5. **Approve the Loan.** The amount and the rules on one screen.
6. **Create Your Trading Wallet.** Hest generates it, shows the private key once, and asks you to confirm you saved it.

Members who joined the whitelist before launch only see the Repost and Reply steps; their loan is already assigned.

## The Amount

The amount is drawn at random when the account is created and shown immediately. Out of every 10 loans, about 3 are **$10**, 5 are between **$20 and $60**, and 2 are higher, rarer as the amount grows, up to a maximum of **$600**.

## The Rules

* **Where you can use it.** On Hest Pools markets only, on Solana and on Robinhood Chain alike. When you open a position you choose **Pay With: Wallet** or **Hest Loan**. A loan position moves no funds on chain: it is an account at Hest, so it needs no deposit and no gas.
* **It is a loan, not a gift.** The loan itself can never be withdrawn. It exists to let you trade without depositing first.
* **Profits above the loan are yours.** Whatever your loan account holds beyond the original loan amount can be requested as a withdrawal.
* **Withdrawal requests take 7 days.** Each request is locked for 7 days, then reviewed by Hest and paid manually to your connected wallet (or, for Google accounts, to your withdrawal address). You see the status and the countdown in Portfolio, and the transaction link once it is paid.
* **One loan per account.** The loan is tied to the account you logged in with.

Loan payouts are funded from Hest Pools revenue. They are variable by nature and depend on real trading results, not a guaranteed return.

## Tracking It

Portfolio shows a **Your Hest Loan** card: the loan amount, the loan account balance, what is currently in open positions, what is withdrawable, and your withdrawal requests with their countdowns.
