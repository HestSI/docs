# Whitelist and Your First Loan

Before trading opens, hest.si runs a whitelist. Joining takes a few minutes, is free, and gives you two things:

* **A numbered whitelist ticket**, yours to keep and share.
* **A Hest Loan**: a trading balance in dollars, assigned to your account, ready to use on Hest Pools the moment trading opens.

## How to Join

On [hest.si](https://hest.si), click **Join Whitelist** and complete the steps in order:

1. **Follow @HestSI** on X.
2. **Join Our Discord.**
3. **Repost Our Post** on X.
4. **Get Your Whitelist Ticket.** Copy or download your ticket card.
5. **Reply to the Post** on X.

When all steps are done, click **All Done** and log in with a wallet or with Google. That account is now on the whitelist, and your loan is assigned to it.

{% hint style="info" %}
Your ticket number is reserved in your browser while you complete the steps. The number becomes yours permanently when you log in at the end, so finish on the same browser you started on.
{% endhint %}

## Your Hest Loan

The amount is drawn at random when you join and shown right away. Out of every 10 loans, about 3 are **$10**, 5 are between **$20 and $60**, and 2 are higher, rarer as the amount grows, up to a maximum of **$600**.

How it works:

* **Where you can use it.** On Hest Pools markets only, starting at launch. When you open a position you choose **Pay With: Wallet** or **Hest Loan**.
* **It is a loan, not a gift.** The loan itself can never be withdrawn. It exists to let you trade without depositing first.
* **Profits above the loan are yours.** Whatever your loan account holds beyond the original loan amount can be requested as a withdrawal.
* **Withdrawal requests take 7 days.** Each request is locked for 7 days, then reviewed by Hest and paid manually to your connected wallet (or, for Google accounts, to your withdrawal address). You see the status and the countdown in Portfolio, and the transaction link once it is paid.
* **One loan per account.** The loan is tied to the account you logged in with when you joined.

Loan payouts are funded from Hest Pools revenue. They are variable by nature and depend on real trading results, not a guaranteed return.

## Tracking It

After launch, Portfolio shows a **Your Hest Loan** card: the loan amount, the loan account balance, what is currently in open positions, what is withdrawable, and your withdrawal requests with their countdowns.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Only connect your wallet on hest.si. Hest never asks for your seed phrase or private key: not in the app, not on Discord, not on X, not by email.
{% endhint %}
